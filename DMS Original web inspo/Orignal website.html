DMS Plumbing - Research and Inspiration

This page documents information researched from the official DMS Plumbing website and was used as inspiration for the student website.

About DMS Plumbing

DMS Plumbing is an owner-managed plumbing company established in 1999 by Darren Michael Smithson.

The company provides plumbing services to residential, commercial, retail and hospitality customers.

DMS Plumbing also provides emergency plumbing services 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Emergency Plumbing

The DMS Plumbing website places strong emphasis on emergency plumbing services and availability.

The company states that emergency plumbers are available 24 hours a day and 7 days a week.

Emergency telephone number: 011 043 5370

Website Research and Inspiration

The DMS Plumbing website was researched to understand how a professional plumbing business presents its services and information to customers.

The research influenced the structure and content planning of the student website, including the use of an About Us page, Services page, Enquiry page and Contact page.

The student website does not copy the original website's source code or visual design. The information was researched and adapted for this academic project.
